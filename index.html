English Speaking & Public Speaking Classes Online — Gurgaon, Delhi NCR, India & Worldwide

Voice & Verve is a premier online English communication coaching founded by Adv. Ritu Khera — a TESOL certified English educator and Supreme Court Advocate with over 20 years of teaching experience. Based in Gurgaon, Haryana, we serve students and professionals across Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bangalore, all of India and worldwide through live online sessions.

Our English speaking classes for kids are designed to help children from Class 4 to Class 10 across all boards — CBSE, ICSE, IB, Cambridge and State Boards — speak with confidence, clarity and fluency. Whether your child hesitates to speak English in school, struggles with presentations, or needs help with grammar, our personalised one-on-one sessions deliver results within weeks.

Our public speaking and personality development coaching helps students overcome stage fright, build a commanding presence, and speak confidently in front of any audience. For working professionals, our interview preparation coaching covers HR rounds, technical interviews, group discussions, and executive communication skills.

We also offer table manners and social etiquette classes for children and adults — teaching dining etiquette, social confidence, body language and poise. All programs include a free 30-minute demo class with no commitment required. Join 300+ students who have transformed their communication skills with Voice & Verve.
