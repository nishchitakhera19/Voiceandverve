The Programs

Four Online Courses.
One transformed you.

Each online English speaking, public speaking and personality development program is hand-built, deeply personal and tailored to your child or your career. Choose by what you need today — switch later if you like. Most students take more than one.

🎤 PROGRAM 01
Public Speaking & Personality Development
From stage fright to standing ovations.
Master the art of public speaking and personality development — body language, pace, pause, projection and the courage to hold a room. Designed for school students, college students and working professionals across India who want their words to land.
Duration12 weeks · 24 sessions
AudienceAge 10 to 60+
Speak confidently to any audience size
Build a personal speaking style
Handle Q&A, panels and impromptu moments
Voice modulation and stage presence
🍽️ PROGRAM 02
Table Manners & Etiquette
Compose yourself at any table, anywhere in the world.
A signature program that goes beyond cutlery — learn poise, posture, dining grace, conversation etiquette, host and guest decorum. Perfect for children stepping into society and adults preparing for global rooms.
Duration6 weeks · workshops
AudienceAge 8 to adult
Master Continental, Indian and casual settings
Confident host & guest behaviour
Social conversation skills
Personal grooming guidance
📖 PROGRAM 03
Basic English Grammar
Tense, voice, narration — finally clear.
A structured, exam-focused English grammar program for kids and students across all boards — CBSE, ICSE, IB, Cambridge and State Board. We rebuild the fundamentals — tenses, voice, narration, modals, error spotting, editing, and writing — with worksheets, mock papers and personal feedback.
DurationSchool year · weekly
AudienceAll Boards · Class 4–10
Score 90+ in English Boards
Crystal-clear grammar fundamentals
Writing skills: essays, letters, articles
Reading comprehension mastery
💼 PROGRAM 04
Interview Preparation
Walk in nervous. Walk out hired.
An intensive, role-specific interview preparation coaching program for working professionals, freshers and MBA aspirants in India. Includes resume polish, mock interviews, HR & technical rounds, behavioural questions, accent neutralisation and post-mock detailed feedback.
Duration4–8 weeks · intensive
AudienceProfessionals & PG aspirants
Crack HR, technical and case rounds
Confident, structured answers
Strong body language on camera & in-person
LinkedIn & resume polish included
